About GovCatalyst — SIH Problem Statement 26136

Problem: Government departments face operational problems that could benefit from innovative startup solutions, but conventional procurement processes are designed for standardised goods and established vendors.

Solution: GovCatalyst provides a structured end-to-end mechanism for challenge identification, startup discovery, eligibility screening, expert evaluation, sandbox/pilot design, milestone-based contracting, performance measurement, payment release, independent validation, and scale-up decisions.

Organization: Government of Maharashtra — Maharashtra State Innovation Society (MSInS), Department of Skills, Employment, Entrepreneurship and Innovation
